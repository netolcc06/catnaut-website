Mission & Vision

01

Mission

To keep the smallest team possible so we can build the games we dream of — without losing ourselves along the way.

02

Vision

To create great Metroidvania (Discovery Games) that explore secrets and the interactions between their systems and the player.

Who We Are

Just some folks who want to make cool games — sustainably.

There's no point working yourself to death and never moving forward. We want to make interesting games with scopes that will actually see the light of day, without sacrificing our lives or our dreams.

We keep our focus tight: Metroidvania and Discovery Games, made by a small crew that cares about every detail.

What Drives Us

Two words, one philosophy.

Exploration

Worlds built to be wandered, questioned and rediscovered — secrets rewarding curiosity, not just completion.

Interaction

Systems that talk to each other and to the player, turning mechanics into moments of genuine discovery.

Sustainable Scope

A small team, honest ambitions, and games designed to actually ship — without burning out the people making them.
